Reactivate My Account

OSCA Magalang · SilverCare Senior Portal

Step 1 — Enter your OSCA ID

Type the OSCA / Senior Citizen ID number from your registration. Your account will be found using this ID, then we will scan your face to confirm it is really you.

Where do I find my OSCA ID?
It is the ID number given to you when you registered at the OSCA office — it is written on your Senior Citizen ID card and shown on your SilverCare profile. Lost it? Please visit the OSCA Magalang office for help.

Step 2 — Scan your face

This account is inactive. Allow the front camera, look straight at it with good light on your face, then press Start Camera and Scan My Face.

This is the photo we will send

Your face matched your registration photo, so this photo is being sent to OSCA staff automatically. You do not need to press anything else.

What happens next?
Your face photo will be compared with the photo from your registration. If it matches, your scanned face and request are sent to OSCA staff for final approval. Once approved, you can log in again. You may also visit the OSCA office anytime for help.

Step 3 — Waiting for review

Your face scan was sent! OSCA staff will now review it and give the final approval. This page updates by itself — please keep it open.
