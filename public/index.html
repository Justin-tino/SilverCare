SILVERCARE

Data Privacy Notice and Consent

In accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 — Data Privacy Act of 2012

SilverCare is a web-based senior citizen records, benefit monitoring, and priority assistance management system developed for the Office of the Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) of Magalang. This Privacy Notice is provided in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

1. Collection of Personal Information

SilverCare may collect and process personal information, including full name, date of birth, address, contact details, senior citizen identification details, supporting documents, photographs for verification, and records related to benefits and assistance. Other sensitive personal information will only be processed when necessary and when permitted by applicable law.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

The collected information will be used for the following purposes:

  • Registration and verification of senior citizens.
  • Management and updating of senior citizen records.
  • Monitoring of benefits, applications, and assistance history.
  • Verification of eligibility and prevention of duplicate or fraudulent claims.
  • Management of queues, appointments, and priority assistance.
  • Preparation of authorized reports for OSCA administration.
3. Protection and Confidentiality

SilverCare shall implement appropriate organizational, physical, and technical security measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse. Access to records shall be restricted to authorized personnel with legitimate official responsibilities.

4. Disclosure and Sharing of Information

Personal information shall only be shared with authorized government personnel or other legitimate recipients when necessary for the stated purposes and when permitted by applicable law. Personal information shall not be disclosed to unauthorized individuals or used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

5. Data Retention and Disposal

Personal information shall be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill its intended purpose or comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and government records retention requirements. When retention is no longer necessary or legally required, information shall be securely disposed of or anonymized, as appropriate.

6. Rights of Data Subjects

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, data subjects have applicable rights, including the right to be informed, access their personal information, request correction of inaccurate information, object to certain processing, and seek remedies for violations of their privacy rights. Other rights, including erasure or blocking and data portability, apply subject to the conditions and limitations provided by law.

Requests concerning personal information may be directed to the authorized OSCA Magalang representative or designated Data Protection Officer through the official contact details provided by the office.

7. Consent and Acknowledgment

By selecting the checkbox below, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Notice and agree to the collection and processing of your personal information for the stated purposes, where consent is the applicable legal basis. Processing that is necessary and authorized by law may rely on another lawful basis. Your acknowledgment does not waive or remove any rights granted to you under Republic Act No. 10173.
