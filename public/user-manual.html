What is SilverCare?

SilverCare is the online system of the Office of the Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) of Magalang, Pampanga. It helps senior citizens:

  • Apply for burial and bedridden assistance, and track monthly pension
  • Book OSCA visits online and get a queue number
  • Keep health records with affiliated barangay health centers
  • Receive reminders and announcements from OSCA staff
Tip: If the text on the screen is too small, hold the Ctrl key and press + to make everything bigger.
Section 2 of 15

Creating your account

Creating an account is free and only needs to be done once. Before you start, prepare:

  • Your Senior Citizen ID details (name, birthdate, address)
  • An email address you can open
  • A phone or computer with a camera
  1. Go to the SilverCare home page and click Create an Account.
  2. Choose Senior Citizen and fill in your name, birthdate, address, and contact details.
  3. Type your email address and a password you will remember.
  4. When asked, take a photo of your face using your phone or computer camera. This photo is used for verification and for reactivating your account later.
  5. Submit your registration. OSCA staff will review it and approve your account.
Important: Ask a family member or visit the OSCA office if you need help registering. Never share your password with strangers.
Section 3 of 15

Logging in

  1. On the home page, click your role: Senior Citizen.
  2. Type your email address and password.
  3. Click Verify & Login.
Senior citizens log in with email + password only (a simpler login designed for older users). Admin and OSCA staff use an extra email code for added security.

Your dashboard & tabs

After logging in you will see big buttons (tabs) at the top. On a small phone the tabs wrap into two rows, so nothing is hidden.

  • DashboardYour account status, next pension release, and recent notifications.
  • ProfileYour personal information and your QR digital ID.
  • BenefitsApply for burial or bedridden assistance and view your claim history.
  • AppointmentsBook OSCA visits, see your queue number, and set medication reminders.
  • Get VerifiedComplete your identity verification to unlock all services.
  • NotificationsMessages and reminders from OSCA. A red dot means something new.
The green menu button (three lines) beside the logo opens the services menu, and the MANUAL button reopens this guide anytime.
Section 5 of 15

Getting verified (KYC)

Verification proves that you are really you. It keeps your benefits safe and unlocks all services.

Prepare before you start:

  • Your Senior Citizen ID - clear photos of the front and back
  • Good lighting for your selfie (remove your hat or eyeglasses if possible)
  • A phone or computer with a working camera
  1. Open the Get Verified tab.
  2. Take a clear photo of your Senior Citizen ID (front and back).
  3. Take a clear selfie following the on-screen guide.
  4. Submit. OSCA staff will review your documents and mark you Verified.
While waiting, some features (like benefits applications) stay locked until you are Verified. A green Verified ✓ badge appears on your dashboard once approved.
Section 6 of 15

Applying for assistance & pension

Monthly pension

Your pension balance and payout history appear on the Dashboard and the Benefits tab. OSCA staff approve payouts - you will be notified when money is released.

Burial / bedridden assistance

  1. Open the Benefits tab.
  2. Choose Burial Assistance or Bedridden Assistance.
  3. Fill in the form and attach the required documents.
  4. Submit and wait for OSCA review. Track your claim status in the same tab.
The system suggests a priority level (High, Medium, or Low) using official rules - for example age, health condition, and disability - but OSCA staff always make the final decision.
Section 7 of 15

Your QR digital ID

Your personal QR code works like a digital ID card.

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Show the QR code at the OSCA office so staff can verify your identity quickly by scanning it.
  3. Optional: press Download Digital ID to save a copy on your phone.
Save the downloaded Digital ID on your phone so you can still show it even without internet connection.
Section 8 of 15

Booking appointments & queue numbers

  1. Open the Appointments tab.
  2. Pick a visit date, time, and purpose of visit (for example: Pension Claim, Health Check-up).
  3. Click Book Appointment. Your queue number appears automatically.
  4. Show your queue number when you arrive at the OSCA office.

Need a different schedule? Use Reschedule in the same tab. You will also get reminders before your visit.

Please arrive about 15 minutes early for your visit - your queue number keeps your spot in line.
Section 9 of 15

Medication reminders & refills

Daily reminders

  1. Open the Appointments tab and find My Medication Reminders.
  2. Click Add Reminder, type the medicine name, dosage, and the time(s) you take it.
  3. When it is time, a reminder pops up. Press Mark as Taken after drinking your medicine.

Requesting a refill

Running out of medicine? Use Request Medication Refill in the same tab. OSCA staff will respond to your request.

Section 10 of 15

Notifications

Open the Notifications tab to read messages from OSCA - for example pension releases, appointment reminders, and medication reminders. A red dot appears on the tab when there is something new.

Section 11 of 15

My account is inactive - how to reactivate it

If your account became inactive, you do not need to register again. You can reactivate it yourself:

  1. On the home page, choose Senior Citizen, then click Account inactive? Reactivate with Face Scan.
  2. Type your OSCA / Senior Citizen ID number, then press Continue to Face Scan.
  3. Press Start Camera, look straight at the camera, then press Scan My Face.
  4. That is all. If your face matches your registration photo, your request is sent to OSCA staff automatically - there is nothing else to press.
  5. Keep the page open - OSCA staff will review and approve your request, and the page will update by itself. Once approved, you can log in again.
No camera, or the scan keeps failing? Visit the OSCA office - staff can help reactivate your account there.
Section 12 of 15

Staying safe & your privacy

  • Never share your password or email codes with anyone. OSCA staff will never ask for them.
  • Your health records are private - only you, your authorized health center, and OSCA staff can see them, and every viewing is recorded.
  • Always press Logout when using a shared phone or computer.

Section 14 of 15

Common questions

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Ask a family member to help you, or visit the OSCA office - staff can reset it for you.

My registration is "Pending". What does that mean?

It means OSCA staff have not reviewed your registration yet. Please wait - you will be notified once approved.

The text on my screen is too small.

On a computer, hold Ctrl and press +. On a phone, pinch to zoom, or increase the text size in your phone settings.

No camera, or the face scan keeps failing?

Make sure you are in a bright place and look straight at the camera. If it still fails, visit the OSCA office - staff can verify or reactivate you in person.

Where do I ask for help in person?

Visit the OSCA Office, Municipality of Magalang, Pampanga, or ask at your barangay health center. Bring your Senior Citizen ID.
